The person behind the build

Mechanical thinking.
Hands-on learning.

I’m a mechanical engineer and content creator interested in CAD, mechanical design, FDM 3D printing and practical prototyping. Curious Layer is where those interests meet.

I enjoy taking a problem from an early sketch to a model, then making a first version and testing it in the real world. The process is rarely perfect on the first try; each iteration is a chance to learn and improve the design.

Alongside design and print services, I share engineering projects and maker content through Curious Layer. The goal is to make useful things and help more people feel confident exploring how they work.

Terms of use

Working with Curious Layer

Scope, price, timing, material availability, delivery arrangements and payment requirements are confirmed individually in writing. A quote is not a production commitment until Curious Layer accepts the project and confirms the agreed scope.

You must have the right to share any model, drawing, logo, reference image or other material you submit. Curious Layer may decline work that appears unlawful, unsafe, infringing or outside its practical manufacturing capability.

Privacy

Your project information

When you contact Curious Layer, we may collect your name, email address, project description, supplied files and the information needed to prepare a quote. This information is used to respond to your enquiry, scope work, deliver any agreed service and maintain necessary business records.

Curious Layer does not sell personal information. Project files and references are handled with care within the practical limits of email and the services used to operate the business. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or other unnecessary sensitive data through a quote request.

For a privacy question or a request concerning your information, email Curiouslayer.official@gmail.com.

Shipping & fulfilment

Arranged locally, one job at a time

Curious Layer currently manages fulfilment locally. The available delivery method, collection option, applicable area, charge and estimated timeline will be confirmed in the individual quote or order confirmation. Delivery terms are not final until agreed in writing.

Please confirm the correct recipient and delivery or collection details before fulfilment. If an item arrives damaged or differs materially from the accepted specification, contact Curious Layer with clear photos and your order details.

Returns & refunds

Custom work deserves a fair review

Custom work is produced to an agreed brief. Before production, the accepted quote should define scope, material, finish, quantity, payment milestones and any revision limits.

Reports about a defective, damaged or materially incorrect order are reviewed against the agreed specification. Any resolution will take account of the order details and applicable consumer rights.

Digital file licence

Use files as agreed

Digital deliverables may include STEP, STL, 3MF, drawings, guides or other formats stated in the applicable quote or product listing. Delivery is arranged by email or a file-sharing link. The included files, version and permitted use should be stated with the delivery.

Unless a written licence says otherwise, do not redistribute, resell, publish, sublicense or represent a Curious Layer digital file as your own. Commercial use and physical-product resale require an explicit written licence. Customers are responsible for checking that a file is suitable for its intended application.

Design, print, test and improve

Engineering and 3D printing services are scoped individually. A quote should identify deliverables, the revision process, timeline, payment terms and responsibility for source files. Feasibility, dimensional tolerance, material behaviour and manufacturing limits vary by project.

Curious Layer does not claim load capacity, safety certification, compliance, life-critical suitability or performance results unless those values have been tested and documented for the exact design and material. Customers should validate final parts for their intended use.
